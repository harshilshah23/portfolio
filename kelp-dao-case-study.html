Kelp DAO: Airdrop Gain Vault Strategy

How replacing paid shills with co-investing mid-tier creators generated $15M in TVL and reduced Cost Per Install by 60%.

TVL Generated
$15,000,000
CPI Efficiency
-60% Cost
Key Mechanic
Skin-in-the-game Staking

01. The Challenge

By mid-2024, Ethereum restaking had reached peak noise. Every liquid restaking protocol was competing for the same deposits, and retail sentiment was fatigued. Paying influencers to post sponsored threads was delivering skyrocketing CPIs and minimal retention. Kelp DAO needed to drive real onchain capital into the Airdrop Gain Vault without looking like hype farmers.

02. Strategic Execution

1. Staking Requirement Before Sponsoring

Mid-tier creators were required to deposit their own capital into the vault before talking about it. When a creator has their own funds locked, their analysis transforms from an ad into an authentic thesis.

2. Speculation Loops

Allowed creators to publish at their natural cadence instead of coordinated blast threads. This fostered organic curiosity in private alpha groups: "Why are these respected researchers staking here?"

3. Long-Term Alignment

Performance was tracked on a live scorecard and tied to future ecosystem governance and airdrop weight, removing the transactional mercenary mindset.

03. Results

  • $15M in TVL directly attributed to the creator syndication system.
  • 60% drop in Cost Per Install (CPI) compared to previous campaigns.
  • Sustained Retention: Funds remained staked long after the initial push because creators were personally invested.
