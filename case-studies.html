RESTAKING • LIQUIDITY VAULTS 2024

Kelp DAO: Airdrop Gain Vault Strategy

How replacing mercenary influencer floods with personal capital staking requirements generated $15M in TVL and reduced Cost Per Install (CPI) by 60%.

The Problem

Ethereum restaking had reached peak noise in mid-2024. Standard paid blast threads led to short-term mercenary deposits that immediately exited after token distributions.

The Solution

Required creators to deposit their own capital into the vault before producing research. Engineered speculation loops: when respected analysts restake, the market follows organically.

The Outcome

Attributed over $15M in TVL directly to the creator syndication system with long-term retention and significant reduction in customer acquisition costs.
