CASE STUDY 01 // RESTAKING GTM

Kelp DAO

AIRDROP GAIN VAULT • 2024
THE STRATEGIC THESIS "Capital allocators do not act on sponsored promotional ads; they act on visible conviction. When creators stake personal capital before publishing, promotional noise transforms into trusted research."
Attributed TVL $15M+
CPI Reduction -60%
Capital Retention High
Role: Growth & Distribution Strategist Domain: Liquid Restaking / DeFi Core Mechanism: Skin-in-the-Game Syndication
01 // THE MARKET FRICTION

The Restaking Fatigue: Why $50K Influencer Retainers Were Burning Capital

In mid-2024, the Ethereum restaking ecosystem was saturated with competing Liquid Restaking Tokens (LRTs). Competing protocols engaged in fierce bidding wars for influencer Twitter/X blast threads. The result was severe audience fatigue: retail stakers ignored generic promotional threads, while the capital that did enter was high-velocity "mercenary liquidity" that immediately unstaked after point settlements. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) was skyrocketing with near-zero long-term capital retention.

02 // STRATEGIC ARCHITECTURE

The Skin-in-the-Game Staking Model

Instead of paying standard upfront promotional fees, we architected a syndication network where credibility was backed by verifiable onchain capital commitment:

• COMPARATIVE DISTRIBUTION MECHANIC
OLD MODEL
Mercenary Promo
Flat-fee promo → Hype tweet → Churn liquidity.
→
OUR MODEL
Personal Stake
Analyst stakes capital → Publishes audit thesis.
→
OUTCOME
Sticky TVL
High-conviction stakers → Sustained yield retention.
  • Personal Deposit Gating: Participating research analysts were required to deposit their own capital into the Airdrop Gain Vault before publishing their analysis.
  • Audit-Level Research Briefs: Replaced promotional hype with technical teardowns explaining yield mechanics, slashing risks, and vault hedging strategies.
  • Milestone-Based Alignments: Structured creator reward allocations tied to 90-day TVL retention rather than raw upfront impressions.
03 // PROVABLE OUTCOMES & TAKEAWAYS

Measurable Onchain Results

  • $15M+ in TVL directly attributed to the creator syndication pipeline during the active vault window.
  • 60% reduction in Cost Per Install (CPI) compared to the protocol's previous campaign benchmarks.
  • Over 70% capital retention post-airdrop distribution, proving that aligned research drives sticky liquidity.

Core Strategic Lesson: In financial technology and decentralized markets, incentive alignment is distribution. Forcing distribution partners to take on verifiable financial risk transforms marketing into credible intellectual capital.

eXchange1

01 // THE STRATEGIC DIAGNOSIS

Overcoming the Discoverability & Amplification Gap

Preparing for a major global and regional expansion from January through June 2026, eXchange1 possessed high-performance matching engines and competitive fee tiers. However, the platform suffered from an acute discoverability gap against heavily marketed incumbent exchanges. Communications across global, regional, and mascot handles were fragmented, lack of operational cadence left critical market volatility windows uncapitalized, and UI/UX copy needed plain-language translation for retail traders.

02 // BRAND & CONTENT OPERATIONS

"We Are One", Catoshi Persona & The 7 Content Pillars

We engineered an integrated brand architecture: anchoring global communications around the master narrative "We Are One", while deploying the mascot persona "Catoshi" to capture informal, high-velocity organic engagement. To maintain institutional rigor, we operationalized all communications into a 7-Pillar Editorial Taxonomy:

Pillar Strategic Purpose Core Content Output
1. Brand & Security Institutional Trust Core mission, regulatory posture, milestone proofs, proof-of-reserves.
2. Culture & Vernacular Community Affinity Trading memes, Web3 lifestyle, high-relatability ecosystem commentary.
3. Product & Features User Conversion Feature releases, order-type teardowns, fee comparisons, UI walkthroughs.
4. Market Updates Daily Habit Formation Real-time macroeconomic analysis, onchain capital flows, liquidity trends.
5. Education & Risk Retail Empowerment Risk management frameworks, order execution guides, security hygiene.
6. Community Moments Engagement & Retention Trader spotlights, competition leaderboards, live interactive AMAs.
7. Thought Leadership Industry Authority Executive essays on market structure, future of digital assets, regulation.
03 // EXECUTION & INTELLIGENCE

130+ Master Comms & Competitive Intelligence Engine

Beyond producing and scheduling 130+ master communications across X, Meta, and LinkedIn, we built continuous competitive intelligence tracking:

  • Workflow Synchronization: Conducted staging dry runs and multi-handle review hierarchies, harmonizing global, regional, and Catoshi channels with zero brand drift.
  • Competitive Sentiment Audits: Tracked conversation gaps and customer support frustrations across competitor exchanges to deploy agile contrast positioning.
  • Amplification Roadmap: Formulated the comprehensive strategy deck presented to the board of directors, establishing the framework for paid amplification, KOL syndication, and institutional PR.
04 // RESULTS & KEY INSIGHT

Institutional Alignment & Scaled Output

  • Unanimous Executive Approval: Secured full board approval on brand guidelines, messaging architectures, and multi-channel roadmaps.
  • Zero-Friction Publishing: Transformed an ad-hoc social setup into a predictable, high-output publishing machine across 3 global networks.
  • Surging Catoshi Organic Engagement: Built an agile persona with significantly higher organic reply-to-impression ratios than legacy corporate handles.

"How We Managed to Launch 20 Projects" Without Narrative Dilution

STAGE 03

03 // EXECUTION & OUTCOMES

Compounding Ecosystem Scale

Managed the end-to-end launch schedules, coordinating directly with partner teams to ensure strict alignment and zero operational delays.

  • Orchestrated the synchronized launch framework for 20 ecosystem projects.
  • Eliminated marketing coordination bottlenecks, reducing time-to-launch from partner commitment.

Core Strategic Lesson: The best developer platforms do not just offer SDKs and grants; they provide go-to-market infrastructure that helps decentralized builders launch like world-class products.

Mira

VERIFIABLE AI TRUST LAYER • 2024
THE STRATEGIC THESIS "Deep tech cannot be sold through buzzwords. It must be positioned around an undeniable commercial reality: autonomous AI agents cannot be trusted with capital without cryptographic output verification."
Category Verifiable AI
Distribution Research-Led
Target Audience AI Devs & Funds
Role: GTM & Technical Distribution Strategist Domain: Verifiable AI / Cryptography / Infrastructure Core Focus: Category Framing & Technical Theses
01 // THE DEEP TECH CHALLENGE

Demystifying Cryptographic Verification for Autonomous Agents

As autonomous AI agents rapidly expand into enterprise and financial environments, AI output hallucination and unverifiable model execution represent critical vulnerabilities. Mira built decentralized infrastructure providing cryptographic verification for AI model outputs. However, the narrative faced a dangerous bifurcation: becoming too mathematically dense for ecosystem partners, or blending into generic "AI + Web3" marketing fluff that serious engineers dismiss.

02 // CATEGORY POSITIONING

Positioning the "Trust Layer for Autonomous Intelligence"

We framed Mira away from compute marketplaces and positioned it strictly as the critical Trust & Verification Primitive:

Generic Narrative (Rejected)

Vague claims about "decentralized AI compute" that confuse developers and attract low-conviction speculative traders.

Verifiable AI Narrative (Adopted)

Framing verification as the indispensable safety rail allowing autonomous AI agents to execute smart contracts and trade capital safely.

  • Technical Research Dossiers: Authored in-depth essays breaking down proof generation, consensus verification for LLMs, and agent execution primitives.
  • High-Signal Syndication: Distributed technical breakdowns directly into developer newsletters, AI researcher communities, and technical X spaces.
  • Developer Workflow Integration: Highlighted SDK integration pathways for autonomous agent builders.
03 // COMPOUNDING CREDIBILITY

Establishing Market Mindshare

  • Established Mira as a recognized pioneer in verifiable decentralized AI infrastructure.
  • Educated ecosystem partners on the critical distinction between raw compute infrastructure and cryptographic verification.
  • Scaled the Mira Discord community to 200,000+ members by crafting and distributing high-conviction narratives.

Core Strategic Lesson: Deep technical infrastructure demands deep respect for the reader's intellect. High-signal research compounds over time where marketing hype cycles quickly fade.
