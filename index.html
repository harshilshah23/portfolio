01. The Challenge

KOL trust in restaking was fragile. Competing protocols were paying inflated fees for fleeting blast threads that generated fast mercenary deposits and immediate churn.

02. The Execution

Required mid-tier researchers to deposit their own capital into the vault before writing. Fostered organic speculation loops: when respected analysts restake, the market pays attention.

03. The Architecture

Deployed performance scorecards tied to future governance and airdrop weight instead of one-off cash stipends. Created sustained, aligned community ownership.
